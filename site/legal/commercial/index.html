Commercial licensing

LoadComposer is available under LCCL-1.0 for the free uses described in LICENSE, including normal internal use by for-profit organizations. A separate commercial license is required when the software itself, access to it, or a substantial implementation of it is monetized.

Typical cases requiring a commercial agreement include:

  • paid hosted access, SaaS, APIs, managed instances, or subscription features;
  • white-label, OEM, private-label, or customer-branded editions;
  • substantial embedding in a paid product, platform, appliance, or service;
  • resale, sublicensing, marketplace distribution, or fees tied to access or use;
  • advertising-, sponsor-, affiliate-, lead-, donation-, or data-funded public hosting; and
  • services whose principal value is implementing, operating, or commercializing LoadComposer for third parties.

Typical cases that do not require a commercial agreement include:

  • internal planning by a company, consultant, carrier, warehouse, manufacturer, or public body;
  • using LoadComposer output in paid logistics or transport work;
  • selling or transporting goods planned with LoadComposer; and
  • free, non-monetized redistribution or public hosting that complies with LCCL-1.0.

For commercial terms, contact licensing@loadcomposer.com with the intended use, deployment model, expected users, branding requirements, and desired support. No rights are granted by an enquiry or by this page; commercial rights arise only from a separate written agreement.

# Roadmap

This roadmap is directional and creates no commitment, deadline, or warranty.

## Post-1.0 validation

- Validate repository and Cloudflare Pages publication from a clean clone.
- Collect focused feedback on compatibility, translations, printing, accessibility, and documentation.
- Obtain professional review of the custom community license, contributor agreement, privacy notice, trademark policy, and consumer-facing disclaimers.
- Resolve defects through separately authorized maintenance releases without
  expanding the operational scope.

## Possible later work

- Additional equipment data with documented provenance and tolerance ranges.
- Stronger saved-project portability without introducing an application backend.
- Accessibility and print refinements supported by regression evidence.
- Carefully scoped loading constraints where a correct model and validation evidence exist.

Weight, balance, axle loads, load securing, route compliance, and regulatory certification are not implied by this roadmap. They require dedicated domain models, qualified review, and separate validation before any implementation claim.

The `v1.0.0` stable release adds no hosted commercial service, new language,
PWA, service worker, backend, analytics, or new distribution channel.

# LoadComposer v1.0.0-rc.4 change summary

Date: 2026-08-03  
Direct baseline: canonical `v1.0.0-rc.3`

RC.4 promotes version metadata; introduces exact nominal and prudential
dimensions for the three 13.60 m trailers; adds the inclusive, non-duplicated
`VERIFICA MISURE` warning in seven languages; makes LICENSE/NOTICE explicitly
inline; separates cache policies; adds Contributor Covenant 2.1; strengthens
direct RC.3 comparison, dimensional, cache, legal and delivery-control tests;
and regenerates all release artifacts.

The placement engine, heuristics and coordinates are unchanged. At equivalent
inputs geometry is `7,542/7,542` identical to RC.3. The corrected reefer profile
uses 13,310 mm as loadable length; 13,410 mm remains descriptive only.

This is a private candidate for verification. It is not a published or finally
approved release.
